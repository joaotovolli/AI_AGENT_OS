# Code of Conduct

AI Agent OS welcomes constructive participation from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Discussions and contributions should remain professional, technical and respectful.

## Expected behaviour

- Be clear, constructive and respectful when discussing code, design decisions and trade-offs.
- Critique ideas and implementations rather than people.
- Keep issues and pull requests relevant to the project and provide enough context for others to reproduce or evaluate a proposal.
- Respect privacy and security boundaries. Never post credentials, tokens, private logs or other sensitive material.
- Accept maintainers' decisions on scope, moderation and project direction, while remaining free to disagree constructively.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, deliberate disruption, doxxing, spam, or publication of another person's sensitive information are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

The repository owner may edit or remove comments, close or lock conversations, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and productive.

For security-sensitive matters, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) rather than posting exploitable details publicly. Other conduct concerns can be raised privately with the repository owner through GitHub.
